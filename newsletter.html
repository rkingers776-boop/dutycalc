What You'll Get Every Monday

Global tariff policy moves fast. Section 301 reviews, FTA phase-ins, anti-dumping investigations, de minimis reform proposals — any one of these can change your landed cost overnight. The Weekly Tariff Briefing distills the noise into what matters for your trade lanes.

Each Issue Covers

  • Rate Changes: Which tariff lines moved, by how much, and when it takes effect
  • Policy Alerts: USTR, CBP, EU Commission, HMRC, CBSA — legislative and regulatory proceedings that could affect your costs
  • Corridor Spotlight: One trade lane analyzed in depth each week (rates, risks, compliance tips)
  • Compliance Corner: Documentation requirements, HS classification rulings, enforcement trends
  • Market Intel: Freight rate movements, port congestion, customs clearance trends

Who It's For

E-commerce sellers, freight forwarders, customs brokers, supply chain managers, import/export traders — anyone whose margins depend on getting duties right. The briefing assumes you know the basics of international trade; we focus on what's changing and what to do about it.

It's Free. No Catch.

No paywall, no upsell, no spam. We built DutyCalc because trade math shouldn't be locked behind software subscriptions. The briefing is the same idea — useful intelligence, delivered clean. If we ever build a paid product, you'll hear about it from the briefing first, but the briefing itself stays free.
