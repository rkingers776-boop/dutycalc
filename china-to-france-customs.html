French Import Duty on Chinese E-Commerce Cargo — The TARIC System

The EU TARIC schedule applies uniformly across all EU member states including France. For Chinese e-commerce goods — household items, electronics, apparel, accessories — the average duty rate is 4.5%. This is a blended estimate across the most common HS chapters. France's Direction Générale des Douanes et Droits Indirects (DGDDI) processes millions of Chinese parcels annually. Here's a real-world example: €1,000 goods + €100 shipping = €1,100 CIF. Duty at 4.5% = €49.50. TVA at 20% on €1,149.50 = €229.90. Total charges: €279.40. Landed cost: €1,379.40.

TVA at 20% — The Big Line Item

TVA (Taxe sur la Valeur Ajoutée) is France's VAT. The standard rate is 20%, calculated on CIF plus duty. In practice, the TVA line is typically 4–5× the duty line — it's the one that catches first-time importers off guard. For TVA-registered businesses in France, import TVA is recoverable via the monthly CA3 déclaration — you pay it at customs, then deduct it next month. For non-EU sellers, IOSS (see below) is the cleaner route.

The €22 Duty De Minimis and the €150 IOSS Split

The EU-wide €22 duty de minimis threshold applies: no customs duty is owed when the goods value is €22 or below. Above that, the full TARIC rate kicks in. But here's the critical update — the TVA de minimis was abolished in July 2021. Every commercial shipment now attracts 20% TVA from €1 upward. The old franchise de TVA (€22 exemption for both duty and TVA) is gone and isn't coming back.

IOSS — Import One-Stop Shop for B2C Under €150

For B2C e-commerce shipments under €150, the Import One-Stop Shop is the preferred route. Register in one EU state (France works), charge French TVA at the point of sale, and remit monthly through the IOSS portal. Goods then clear French customs TVA-free. This is a huge customer-experience win — no surprise TVA bill at delivery, no carrier handling fees, way fewer refused packages. Over €150, standard import procedures apply and TVA is collected at the border. See also our calculators for Germany and the Netherlands.

French Customs Entry Points and Clearance

Most Chinese e-commerce cargo enters through Paris Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) for air freight, or Le Havre / Fos-sur-Mer for sea freight. Clearance runs through the DELTA electronic system (Dédouanement en Ligne par Traitement Automatisé). You'll need a French or EU EORI number — apply through douane.gouv.fr, it's free, and takes about a week. Express parcels at CDG typically clear within hours. Sea freight at Le Havre: 1–2 days for standard entry. French customs has been investing heavily in automated parcel processing — throughput at CDG has improved significantly since 2024.

  • 8517 12: Smartphones and mobile handsets
  • 8471 30: Laptops, tablets, and portable computing devices
  • 9503 00: Toys, games, and recreational articles
  • 6110 20: Cotton knitwear — sweaters, hoodies, t-shirts
  • 9405 40: LED lighting fixtures and lamps
  • 4202 92: Bags, cases, and backpacks with textile outer surface

France-specific highlight: 6204 43 (synthetic dresses) is a big category for Paris fashion e-commerce imports.

French Product Compliance Requirements

CE marking is mandatory for electronics. EN 71 applies to toys. Textiles need fiber composition labels in French (étiquetage de composition). The DGCCRF actively monitors e-commerce imports and can seize non-compliant goods on the spot. Packaging waste rules: if you ship into France commercially, you may need to register with Citeo, the eco-contributor scheme. Since 2022, all consumer products sold in France must carry the Triman recycling logo and sorting instructions on the packaging.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the duty rate from China to France?

The average TARIC rate is 4.5% for general e-commerce goods. Actual rates range from 0% (ITA electronics) to 12% (certain textiles). The 4.5% figure is a realistic blended estimate — it won't be exact for every HS code, but it's close enough for most shipment planning.

Do I need IOSS to sell from China to French customers?

For B2C sales under €150, IOSS isn't mandatory but is strongly recommended. Without it, your customer pays TVA plus a carrier handling fee (€10–25) at delivery — and that leads straight to refused packages and chargebacks. With IOSS, the customer sees the all-in price at checkout and gets a clean delivery.

How long does French customs clearance take?

Express consignments through CDG courier hubs typically clear within hours. Sea freight at Le Havre: 1–2 working days for standard entry. Inspections add 2–5 days. DGDDI clearance is generally fast relative to some other EU member states — the automated DELTA system handles the vast majority of entries without manual intervention.

Do I need a fiscal representative in France to import from China?

If you're non-EU based and not registered under IOSS, you typically need a fiscal representative for TVA purposes. If you use IOSS, you don't — the IOSS registration handles it. If you're EU-based but not French, your home-country EORI works throughout the EU under the single-market customs rules.
