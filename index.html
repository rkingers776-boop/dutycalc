Trade Corridor Directory

Per-market duty rates, VAT logic, and compliance checkpoints — organized the way importers actually think.

China → US (Electronics)

2.5%

Most consumer electronics at 2.5% or zero under the WTO ITA. Section 301 tail risk on accessories.

China → US (Apparel)

16.5%

Highest-volume, highest-risk. Section 301 stacked on MFN. USTR exclusion reviews quarterly.

China → UK (Electronics)

2.5% · 20%

Low rates survived Brexit. VAT from the first pound — no de minimis for VAT on commercial imports.

China → UK (Apparel)

12% · 20%

One of the UK's highest rates. A £1,000 CIF shipment lands at roughly £1,344 with tax-on-tax.

China → Germany

4.2% · 19%

Default FBA entry point. Article 23 deferral delays VAT outlay by 45-60 days.

China → France

4.5% · 20%

CDG handles bulk of e-commerce parcels. IOSS makes sub-€150 B2C straightforward.

China → Italy (Textiles)

12% · 22%

Highest EU VAT rate. Aggressive origin enforcement on textiles and leather.

China → Netherlands

3.5% · 21%

Rotterdam bonded warehousing defers both duty and VAT until goods leave.

China → Japan

3.5% · 10%

RCEP Year 4 phase-down in effect. ¥10,000 de minimis, PSE cert required for electronics.

China → Australia

0-5% · 10%

ChAFTA eliminated duties on most goods. AUD $1,000 de minimis — most generous anywhere.

China → Canada

6.5% · 5%

CAD $20 de minimis is the stingiest anywhere. No FTA with China — full MFN on everything.

Korea → US (Electronics)

0% KORUS

KORUS FTA: zero duty on virtually all electronics. Just need an origin cert.

Canada → US

0% USMCA

Most goods enter duty-free. Lumber is the exception — AD/CVD up to 18%. No US VAT on imports.

Mexico → US

0% USMCA

Autos, produce, electronics, medical devices. CBP scrutinizing nearshoring origin claims.

Taiwan → US (Semiconductors)

0% ITA

Chips and ICs enter duty-free under WTO ITA. No FTA needed. Non-ITA electronics 0–5% MFN.
